# 📜 Shinobi Roster Manual

This document details the character traits, elemental affinities, battle styles, and signature Jutsus of all 14 shinobi featured in the simulator.

---

## 👥 Selectable Playable Presets

### 1. Naruto Uzumaki (Uzumaki Clan)
* **Chakra Affinity**: Wind (Primary)
* **Battle Style**: Deceptive Brawler & Infiltration Tactician. Naruto uses massive reserves of chakra to overwhelm opponents with numbers (Shadow Clones), utilizing them as distractions, sensory networks, or kinetic launchers. He relies heavily on mid-to-close-range combat, closing gaps with explosive speed and stamina.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Shadow Clone Jutsu` (Taijutsu): Summons clones to strike in kinetic cohesion.
  - `Rasengan` (Ninjutsu): A spinning sphere of concentrated chakra in the palm.
  - `Rasenshuriken` (Ninjutsu - Wind): Wind-infused spinning blade of chakra that shreds target's cells.
  - `Tailed Beast Bomb` (Ninjutsu - Wind): Condenses positive and negative chakra into a massive energy blast.

### 2. Sasuke Uchiha (Uchiha Clan)
* **Chakra Affinity**: Lightning & Fire (Primary)
* **Battle Style**: Flawless Speed & Spatial Dominance. Sasuke combines high-speed physical combat with elite Dojutsu (Sharingan/Rinnegan) perception. He reads movements, casts instantaneous Genjutsu, and manipulates space itself to swap positions.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Fireball Jutsu` (Ninjutsu - Fire): Uchiha trademark fire breath.
  - `Chidori` (Ninjutsu - Lightning): Concentrates lightning chakra into the hand, chirping like a thousand birds.
  - `Kirin` (Ninjutsu - Lightning): Guides natural lightning down in the shape of Kirin.
  - `Amaterasu` (Ninjutsu - Fire): Casts inextinguishable black flames.
  - `Amenotejikara` (Genjutsu): Swaps places using Rinnegan space-time manipulation, stunning the target.

### 3. Kakashi Hatake (Copy Ninja)
* **Chakra Affinity**: Lightning (Primary)
* **Battle Style**: Adaptive Counter-Tactician. Kakashi evaluates an enemy's kit within seconds and counters using precise elements or psychological traps. He utilizes stealth and physical clones to scout danger before committing to a lethal, high-speed strike.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Lightning Blade` (Ninjutsu - Lightning): Lightning focused for a high-speed piercing jab.
  - `Earth Spike` (Ninjutsu - Earth): Raises sharp rock spikes from the ground.
  - `Water Dragon Bullet` (Ninjutsu - Water): Shapes a giant water dragon to crush the opponent.
  - `Kamui` (Genjutsu): Warps space around target's limbs, causing damage and a heavy stun.
  - `1000 Years of Death` (Taijutsu): A painful finger-jab from behind.

### 4. Gaara (Kazekage Clan)
* **Chakra Affinity**: Earth, Wind (Magnet Release)
* **Battle Style**: Absolute Defense & Long-Range Implosion. Gaara rarely moves from a fixed position. He controls the battlefield by turning the surrounding ground into sand, crushing opponents under massive pressure, or trapping them in inescapable constructs from a distance.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Sand Coffin & Burial` (Ninjutsu - Earth): Binds target in sand and implodes it under extreme pressure.
  - `Sand Tsunami` (Ninjutsu - Earth): Creates a massive tidal wave of sand to crush opponents.
  - `Wind Cutter` (Ninjutsu - Wind): Slashes target with compressed wind blades.

### 5. Itachi Uchiha (Uchiha Clan)
* **Chakra Affinity**: Fire (Primary)
* **Battle Style**: Psychological Warfare & Lethal Efficiency. Itachi dismantles opponents before physical combat even begins. He relies heavily on visual Genjutsu to shatter an enemy's mind in a fraction of a second, using crows and black flames to end fights instantly.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Tsukuyomi` (Genjutsu): Traps target in a nightmare world where time is controlled, heavily stunning them.
  - `Amaterasu` (Ninjutsu - Fire): Casts inextinguishable black flames.
  - `Crow Clone Jutsu` (Genjutsu): Disperses into a flock of crows to confuse and stun the enemy.

### 6. Rock Lee (Taijutsu Specialist)
* **Chakra Affinity**: None (Lee is incapable of performing Ninjutsu or Genjutsu)
* **Battle Style**: Pure, High-Velocity Taijutsu. Lee relies strictly on peak physical conditioning, muscle memory, and extreme speed. By removing training weights or opening the Eight Gates, he bypasses human physical limitations.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Drunken Fist` (Taijutsu): Lee's unpredictable, high-velocity drunken movements.
  - `Leaf Hurricane` (Taijutsu): Spinning low kick sweeping the opponent off their feet.
  - `Primary Lotus` (Taijutsu): A dangerous pile-driver that causes fatigue (HP cost) to the user.
  - `Hidden Lotus` (Taijutsu): Opening the Five Gates to strike at extreme speeds, severely exhausting the user.

## ⚔️ Single Battle CPU Opponents

### 7. Konohamaru Sarutobi
* **Chakra Affinity**: Wind & Fire (Sarutobi Clan traits)
* **Battle Style**: Hybrid Vanguard. He utilizes tactical diversions heavily inspired by Naruto (Shadow Clones) combined with heavy elemental fire release, closing in for high-impact point-blank finishes.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Shadow Clone Jutsu` (Taijutsu): Multi-clone kinetic strike.
  - `Rasengan` (Ninjutsu - Wind): Spinning sphere of concentrated chakra.
  - `Burning Ash` (Ninjutsu - Fire): Spits gunpowder ash and ignites it, causing an explosion.

### 8. Kiba Inuzuka
* **Chakra Affinity**: Earth
* **Battle Style**: Feral Fused Taijutsu. Kiba fights in absolute tandem with his ninken, Akamaru. He uses beast-mimicry techniques to increase his senses and speed, turning his own body into a high-speed drill to tear through defenses.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Fang Over Fang` (Taijutsu): Kiba and Akamaru spin like drills to pierce the opponent.
  - `Two-Headed Wolf` (Taijutsu): Transforms into a giant multi-headed beast, crushing the enemy.
  - `Dynamic Entry` (Taijutsu): Flying jump-kick.

### 9. Shikamaru Nara (Nara Clan)
* **Chakra Affinity**: Earth (Yin Release)
* **Battle Style**: Long-Range Strategic Mastermind. Shikamaru avoids direct physical combat. He treats the battlefield like a shogi board, baiting enemies into specific positions where his shadow can capture and strangle them from afar.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Shadow Possession` (Genjutsu): Binds the target's shadow, freezing them in place (stun).
  - `Shadow Strangle` (Genjutsu): Strangles the target with physical shadow bindings (stun).

### 10. Asuma Sarutobi
* **Chakra Affinity**: Wind & Fire
* **Battle Style**: Close-Quarters Power Striker. Asuma channels sharp wind-natured chakra into his specialized trench knives, vastly extending their cutting range and lethality. He backs this up with exploding ash clouds.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Flying Swallow` (Taijutsu): Infuses wind chakra into trench knives to extend blade length.
  - `Burning Ash` (Ninjutsu - Fire): Ignites a cloud of gunpowder ash.
  - `Dynamic Entry` (Taijutsu): Basic combat kick.

### 11. Orochimaru
* **Chakra Affinity**: Wind (Masters all elements)
* **Battle Style**: Attrition, Modification, & Immortality. Orochimaru fights with an elastic, highly altered body. He relies on snake summonings, hidden poison blades, and reanimation techniques to wear down opponents.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Snake Hands` (Ninjutsu - Earth): Summons snakes from sleeves to constrict the target.
  - `Edo Tensei` (Ninjutsu - Earth): Reanimates past warriors to strike the opponent down.
  - `Temple of Nirvana` (Genjutsu): Causes a rain of feathers, putting the target to sleep (stun).

### 12. Madara Uchiha (Uchiha Clan)
* **Chakra Affinity**: Fire (Primary)
* **Battle Style**: Absolute Total War. Madara possesses god-tier mastery over everything from base Taijutsu to planetary-scale Ninjutsu. He can wipe out entire armies using fire style techniques, Susanoo defenses, or meteors.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Majestic Destroyer Flame` (Ninjutsu - Fire): Uchiha style total-war flame wall.
  - `Tengai Shinsei` (Ninjutsu - Earth): Drops a giant meteor from the atmosphere onto the arena.
  - `Susanoo Strike` (Taijutsu): Strikes target with Susanoo arms.

## 🏆 Tournament Mode Opponents

### 13. Temari
* **Chakra Affinity**: Wind (Primary)
* **Battle Style**: Long-Range Aerial Zoning. Using her Giant Folding Fan, Temari acts as a heavy-artillery wind user. She dictates battlefield spacing, deflecting projectiles and creating impenetrable slicing wind screens.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Wind Scythe Jutsu` (Ninjutsu - Wind): Slicing wind gusts.
  - `Quick Beheading Dance` (Ninjutsu - Wind): Summons Kamatari the weasel to slash the target.

### 14. Neji Hyuga (Hyuga Clan)
* **Chakra Affinity**: Wind / Earth / Water
* **Battle Style**: Flawless Close-Range Interdiction. Utilizing the Byakugan’s near 360-degree vision, Neji target-strikes the precise chakra pathways (tenketsu) of his opponents using the Gentle Fist to block chakra, backed by absolute rotational defenses.
* **Signature Jutsus**:
  - `Eight Trigrams 64 Palms` (Taijutsu): Gentle Fist strikes 64 tenketsu pathways, shutting down chakra.
  - `Revolving Heaven` (Taijutsu): Releases chakra from pores while spinning rapidly, creating absolute defense.
